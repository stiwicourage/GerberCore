# Gerber Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Gerber is an open project focused on building a maintainable, secure and pleasant interactive DevOps multitool through shared ownership and strong collaboration.

We are committed to a community that is welcoming, respectful and constructive for everyone, regardless of background, identity or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

Members of the community are expected to:

- Be respectful, professional and constructive in all interactions
- Assume good intent while still giving honest feedback
- Communicate clearly and focus on helping others succeed
- Prefer collaboration over gatekeeping
- Share knowledge openly and help reduce friction for contributors
- Accept feedback with maturity and respond professionally
- Take responsibility for the quality and long-term maintenance of what they contribute
- Respect established quality standards, documentation and testing practices

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind
- Personal attacks, insults or hostile language
- Trolling, inflammatory comments or repeated disruptive behavior
- Dismissing others in a disrespectful or demeaning way
- Gatekeeping, exclusionary conduct or unnecessary barriers to contribution
- Bad-faith arguments, deliberate misrepresentation or repeated unconstructive conflict
- Publishing private, sensitive or confidential information, including credentials, without permission
- Any conduct that makes collaboration unsafe, hostile or unnecessarily difficult

## Shared Ownership and Responsibility

We welcome new ideas, including ideas that introduce new directions. A contribution that expands the project may still be accepted, with ownership: contributors may be asked to take ongoing responsibility for the areas they introduce. Innovation should not be blocked, but responsibility must remain clear.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Gerber community spaces, including GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation and code reviews. It also applies when an individual represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the maintainers using GitHub's [private reporting](https://github.com/stiwicourage/GerberCore/security/advisories/new).

Please include as much relevant information as possible: what happened, where and when it happened, who was involved and any supporting context. Do not include credentials or other secrets.

All reports are reviewed seriously and handled as promptly and fairly as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

- a private warning
- a request to stop the behavior
- removal of content
- temporary restriction from participation
- a permanent ban from some or all community spaces

Enforcement decisions are made to protect the health, safety and long-term sustainability of the community.
